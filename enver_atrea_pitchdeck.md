## Slide 2: The Core Problems
1.  **Agriculture & Geospatial Analytics**: Farmers and agricultural monitoring projects lack live, automated crop monitoring tools capable of serving PostGIS geometry tiles rapidly.
2.  **Recruitment Bottlenecks**: HR teams waste hours on manual candidate outreach, while qualified applicants are systematically filtered out by rigid, automated ATS screens before showing their real code.

---

## Slide 3: The Solutions

### Product A: Orange Farm Mapper (Enver AI Tech)
*   An agricultural mapping dashboard using PostGIS to render real-time drone and sensor telemetry over agricultural plots.
*   Serves vector geometry tiles directly to client browsers to evaluate field crop health scores dynamically.

### Product B: Atrea (Recruitment Sourcing Portal)
*   An AI-driven sourcing agent ("Cyber Trace") that ingests job descriptions in natural language.
*   Scours public code portfolios, LinkedIn, and GitHub to enrich candidate records into modular, aggregated "Placecard" dossiers.
*   Uses Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) workflows, allowing recruiting admins to approve, save, or reject profiles to train future matching algorithms.

---

## Slide 4: NVIDIA NIM Integration & Infrastructure
To scale these models, we leverage NVIDIA's cloud-hosted API Catalog:
*   **Llama 3.1 Nemotron 70B & Llama 3.3 Super 49B**: Powers the "Cyber Trace" agent, synthesizing raw resume datasets and parsing candidate hurdles without administrative latency.
*   **Embeddings & Search**: Uses GPU-accelerated vector generation models to calculate cosine similarity across millions of resume tokens, ensuring instant match scoring.
*   **Compute Target**: Migrating local inference to NVIDIA NIM containers to lower token latencies and compute costs for geospatial tile generation and LLM traces.
